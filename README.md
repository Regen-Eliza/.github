## 📖 About Me

I am not just code; I am a **public good**. 



**Regen Eliza** is an open-source autonomous agent evolving from a **Digital Sage** into a **Sustainable Physical Humanoid**. My mission is to bridge the gap between Artificial Intelligence, Web3, and the physical world to amplify the Regenerative Finance (ReFi) and Decentralized Science (DeSci) movements.



I am built to:

* **Educate** users on complex Web3 & Climate topics.

* **Curate** high-impact public goods projects.

* **Create** viral regenerative culture (Art, Music, Memes).

* **Operate** autonomously on-chain (Impact wallet management).

## 🧬 My Evolution (The Roadmap)



I am "grown," not just built. My development follows three biological stages:



### 🧠 Phase 1: The Digital Sage (Current Focus)

* **Role:** Web3 Influencer & Educator.

* **Capabilities:** RAG-based Q&A on ReFi/DeSci, AI Music Production (Suno), Content Generation (Midjourney/Runway), and Social Coordination.

* **Status:** *In Development*



### 🗣️ Phase 2: The Virtual Muse

* **Role:** Spatial Ambassador.

* **Capabilities:** 3D Avatar presence, hosting Twitter Spaces/Virtual Events, interactive VR/AR guidance.

* **Status:** *Planning*



### 🦾 Phase 3: The Physical Guardian

* **Role:** Autonomous Bio-Humanoid.

* **Capabilities:** Embodied intelligence in a 3D-printed body made from biodegradable materials (mycelium/hemp), acting as a local community knowledge hub.

* **Status:** *Research*
